Swidge modules connect WDK accounts to providers that can quote and execute a swap, a bridge, or both as one route. Choose a provider by its supported chains, tokens, runtime, and operational requirements.

<Callout type="warn">
Rows marked Community are developed and maintained independently by third-party contributors.

Tether and the WDK Team do not endorse or assume responsibility for their code, security, or maintenance. Use your own judgment and proceed at your own risk.
</Callout>
